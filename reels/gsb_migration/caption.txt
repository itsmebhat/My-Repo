Kamath. Pai. Shenoy. Prabhu… why did they ALL leave Goa? 🙏

🌙 1560: devotees carried Lord Mangesh out of Goa in secret, to safety across the river, in the Bijapur Sultan's land
⛪ 1567: about 300 temples in Bardez gone, rituals banned under Portuguese rule
🚶 Konkani Saraswat families moved south: Karwar, Udupi, Kudla, Kochi… and their gods came with them
🛕 Karkala's Padu Tirupati (1537, tradition says) and Kudla's Sri Venkataramana Temple, whose teru gave us the name CAR STREET 😄
🌾 Rice trade with the Bunts, then Canara Bank and Syndicate Bank
🔁 Twist: many Kudla families still travel back to Goa… to visit their kuladevata

One original surname, two paths. Together they made South Kanara great ❤️
