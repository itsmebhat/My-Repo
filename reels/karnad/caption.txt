He gave away everything for India's freedom… and his ooru still has no board in his name 🇮🇳

Karnad is not just a surname. It's an ooru right next to Mulki, on the Shambhavi river 🌊
Old records call it the port of "Mulki or Karnad" 😅 Always the "or"!
1705: the Portuguese signed a treaty to collect customs duty here, on rice 🌾
24 Feb 1784, Ash Wednesday: Mulki's Catholics were gathered at Markunje near Karnad and marched to Srirangapatna ⛓️
1895: those families' descendants blessed St Joseph's Church, Karnad ⛪
And the name Karnad Sadashiva Rao, "the Gandhi of the South", carries this ooru everywhere.
