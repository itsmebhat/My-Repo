Kasaragod was almost Karnataka's… it was decided by one line in a government report 📜

In 1955, about 72% spoke Malayalam. Kannada representatives agreed Kerala could take the land up to the Chandragiri river, but the whole taluk went to Kerala as splitting it was "administratively inconvenient".
In 1967 the Mahajan Commission said the north should go to Mysore. Kerala rejected it. 59 years later, it's still on paper 😶
