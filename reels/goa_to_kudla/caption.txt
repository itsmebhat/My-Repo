Fernandes Prabhu. D'Souza Kamath. Saldanha Shet. Why do Mangalorean Catholics carry Konkani Hindu surnames? 🤔

⛪ Goan families became Catholic but kept their old family names
🌊 Inquisition, taxes, droughts, Bijapur and Maratha wars pushed them south. In 1747 alone, a Goa report recorded 5,000 Christians leaving
👑 The Keladi kings welcomed them as farmers who could save flooded fields
😂 Some even went back to Goa… then Kudla again. Goa or Kudla? Still can't decide!
🙏 In 1784, around 7,000 escaped Tipu's captivity, many hidden by Hindu neighbours. Kirem church still honours three Hindu families every year. That's Tulunadu ❤️
😮 Twist: Christians were already in Mangalore in 1502 and met Vasco da Gama!
