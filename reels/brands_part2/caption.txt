Syndicate Bank started with just ₹8,000… less than your phone EMI 😅🏦

Our tiny coast gave India FIVE banks, all born to fight one villain: the village moneylender 😈
🕌 1906, Udupi: Haji Abdulla Saheb starts the Canara Banking Corporation → Corporation Bank
📜 1906, Mangaluru: Ammembal Subba Rao Pai starts the Canara Hindu Permanent Fund → Canara Bank
🇮🇳 1924, Mangaluru: Swadeshi spirit → Karnataka Bank
🧵 1925, Udupi: 3 men, ₹8,000, for struggling weavers → Syndicate Bank
🌾 1931: A. B. Shetty and Bunt families open a bank on Vijayadashami → Vijaya Bank

They called it the cradle of Indian banking… and then 3 of them disappeared on April 1st 🤡
Twist: Syndicate started as "Canara" and ended up inside Canara, 95 years later 🏠
