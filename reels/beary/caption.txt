A Malayali hears Tulu. A Tuluva hears Malayalam. Both are wrong 😄 It's BEARY, made right here in Tulunadu ❤️

🗣️ Words from Malayalam, grammar from Tulu
🐟 vala → bale, vayaru → bayaru: Tulu caught the V and threw it back as a B!
🐢 aama → aame: the tortoise lost one letter walking from Kerala to Kudla
🕌 Scholars studied in Ponnani, Malabar, in Malayalam
💰 Byara = trade in Tulu. In 1891, about 97% of South Canara's traders were "Beary", a work name, not a religious one
🏙️ Kudla was once Maikala, and today Bengaluru has the Bearys Group 😎
