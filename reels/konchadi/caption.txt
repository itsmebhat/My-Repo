Her temple in Goa was destroyed in 1567… and 420 years later she got a new home in Konchadi 🙏

🛕 Mahalasa Narayani: her Velham temple in Goa was destroyed by the Portuguese in 1567
🌊 Her idol was moved to Mardol to keep her safe
🏠 In Mangaluru she was first worshipped at Nagesh Kamath's home
🧱 9 Feb 1985: Kashi Math swamiji laid the foundation at Konchadi
🪔 30 Nov 1987: the Goddess was installed
🪙 Twist: they still asked Goa. 11 gold coins were offered on the Mardol oracle's directions 😄
